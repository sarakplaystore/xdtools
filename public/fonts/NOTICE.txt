Fonts fetched on demand
-----------------------
When a PDF uses a font that is not listed above, the editor can download
it from the Khmer Software Initiative archive or from Google Fonts (both
via GitHub). Those files stay under their own licences (LGPL / OFL /
Apache 2.0, as stated in each font).
